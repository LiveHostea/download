MasalahnyaThe problem

Live seorang diri memang huru-hara.

Selling live on your own is chaos.

Anda kena tunjuk produk, layan penonton, dan baca chat yang laju gila, semua serentak.

You have to show products, entertain viewers and read a chat that scrolls too fast, all at the same time.

💨

Chat terlalu laju

Chat moves too fast

"Lock 3!" tenggelam antara 50 mesej "cantiknya sis" dan emoji. Pembeli rasa diabaikan dan lari.

"Lock 3!" gets buried under 50 "so pretty!" messages and emojis. Buyers feel ignored and leave.

👀

Asyik tengok skrin

Eyes glued to the screen

Setiap kali anda tunduk baca chat, anda hilang eye contact dengan penonton, dan tenaga live jatuh.

Every time you look down to read chat, you lose eye contact with viewers and the energy drops.

📦

Stok terlebih jual

Overselling stock

Sepuluh orang lock barang yang tinggal tiga. Lepas live, anda kena minta maaf dan buat refund.

Ten people lock an item with three left. After the live, you're stuck apologising and refunding.

Cara ia berfungsiHow it works

Co-host digital yang tak pernah penat.

A digital co-host that never gets tired.

Pasang sekali di laptop. Setiap kali live, LiveHostea buat empat perkara ini secara automatik.

Set it up once on your laptop. Every time you go live, LiveHostea does these four things automatically.

👂

Baca chat

Reads the chat

Extension pelayar membaca chat live yang terpapar di skrin anda, termasuk nama pembeli.

A browser extension reads the live chat shown on your screen, including each buyer's name.

Faham niat

Asingkan "lock", tanya stok, tanya harga dan aduan daripada spam dan sembang biasa, dalam BM dan English.

Separates locks, stock and price questions, and complaints from spam and small talk, in Malay and English.

Permintaan penting dibacakan ke earbud anda dengan kelajuan 1.0x hingga 1.5x. Penonton tak dengar.

Important requests are read into your earbud at 1.0x to 1.5x speed. Viewers don't hear it.

Kad produk, harga dan "🔒 Locked untuk @nama" muncul di live anda melalui OBS.

Product cards, prices and "🔒 Locked for @name" appear on your stream through OBS.

Soalan lazimFAQ

Ada soalan?

Questions?

Betul ke percuma?Is it really free?

Ya. Community Edition percuma untuk penjual, termasuk untuk jualan perniagaan anda sendiri. Yang tak dibenarkan ialah menjual semula LiveHostea atau membuat produk pesaing daripadanya. Ciri premium akan datang dalam LiveHostea Cloud.

Yes. The Community Edition is free for sellers, including for your own business. What isn't allowed is reselling LiveHostea or building a competing product from it. Premium features will come in LiveHostea Cloud.

Platform mana yang disokong?Which platforms are supported?

YouTube Live dan Twitch disokong terus. TikTok Live dan Shopee Live disokong secara percubaan kerana laman mereka kerap berubah. Untuk platform lain, anda boleh beritahu extension di mana chat berada (lihat panduan).

YouTube Live and Twitch are supported out of the box. TikTok Live and Shopee Live have best-effort support because their pages change often. For other platforms, you can tell the extension where the chat is (see the guide).

Saya live guna telefon. Boleh guna?I go live from my phone. Can I use it?

Boleh. Stream seperti biasa dari telefon, dan buka halaman chat live yang sama di Chrome pada laptop. LiveHostea baca chat dari laptop dan bisikkan ke earbud yang disambung ke laptop.

Yes. Stream from your phone as usual and open the same live chat page in Chrome on a laptop. LiveHostea reads the chat on the laptop and whispers into an earbud connected to it.

Perlu pandai IT ke?Do I need to be technical?

Tidak. Muat turun aplikasi untuk Windows atau Mac dan pasang seperti aplikasi biasa; tiada terminal atau kod. Selepas itu, setiap kali live cuma buka aplikasi LiveHostea. Satu-satunya langkah tambahan ialah memasang extension Chrome untuk membaca chat, dan panduan kami terangkan setiap langkah.

No. Download the app for Windows or Mac and install it like any other app; no terminal or code. After that, just open LiveHostea each time you go live. The only extra step is adding the Chrome extension that reads your chat, and our guide walks through every step.

Adakah ia melanggar terma platform live?Does it break the live platform's terms?

LiveHostea hanya membaca chat yang sudah terpapar di pelayar anda, sama seperti anda membacanya sendiri. Ia tidak menghantar mesej, tidak log masuk bagi pihak anda dan tidak memanggil API platform. Kami tetap cadangkan anda semak terma platform anda sendiri.

LiveHostea only reads chat that is already shown in your browser, just like you reading it yourself. It doesn't post messages, log in on your behalf or call the platform's API. We still recommend checking your own platform's terms.

Data pelanggan saya disimpan di mana?Where is my customer data stored?

Di komputer anda sendiri, dalam fail pangkalan data tempatan. Tiada apa yang dihantar ke pelayan kami. Jika anda pasang webhook, data hanya dihantar ke alamat yang anda pilih.

On your own computer, in a local database file. Nothing is sent to our servers. If you set up webhooks, data only goes to the addresses you choose.

Set up in 5 minutes. Rehearse in the sandbox first, then go live for real.
